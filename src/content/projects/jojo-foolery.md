Jojo Foolery is a Jojo's Bizzare Adventure style battlegrounds game inspired by games such as Jujutsu Shenanigans, The Strongest Battlegrounds, Strikeborn and other prominent games within the battlegrounds genre.

For the moment I am personally responsible for (almost) EVERYTHING regarding Jojo Foolery. Scripting, Modelling, Building, Animating and VFX is all done by me.

Character concepts and general concepting however is done by H3 :J
